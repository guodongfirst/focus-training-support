Privacy Policy

Focus Training · Effective October 15, 2026

Overview

Focus Training is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, or backend service. This policy describes the current App Store version of the app.

Information stored on your device

The app may store settings and training progress locally on your device, including language, appearance, sound preferences, and exercise history used to provide app functionality. This information is not uploaded to the developer.

Data we do not collect

The current version does not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, health data, payment information, identifiers, diagnostics, or account credentials. It does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not display advertising.

Deleting data

You can delete locally stored Focus Training data by deleting the app from your device. Because the app has no account or server-side profile, the developer has no remote user record to delete.

External services

If you choose to open this support website, send an email, or submit a GitHub issue, the relevant service provider processes that activity under its own privacy policy. Avoid including sensitive information in a support request.

Children's privacy

Focus Training does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If a future app version introduces analytics, advertising, cloud services, or other data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
